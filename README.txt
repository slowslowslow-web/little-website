YOUR GIFT WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html = the website
- style.css = the design
- script.js = interactions

BEFORE UPLOADING:
1. Put your MP3 in this folder and name it: song.mp3
2. Put your artwork in this folder and name it: artwork.jpg
3. In index.html, replace YOUR SONG TITLE and YOUR NAME.
4. Replace WRITE YOUR LETTER HERE with your real letter.
5. To use your artwork, replace the .art-placeholder div in index.html with:
   <img src="artwork.jpg" alt="Artwork">
   Then add this to style.css:
   .art-placeholder img { width:100%; height:100%; object-fit:cover; }

The website is designed for GitHub Pages and does not require a backend.
